# Setting up to commute as a new resident

For daily commuting in Dubai, a new resident typically needs:

1. **A nol card** — the single card for metro, bus, tram and marine transport. A personalised (Blue) card is worth registering if you commute daily, because it protects your balance and supports concessions.
2. **An understanding of fare zones** — nol fares depend on how many zones a journey crosses, not distance. If your commute is regular, a monthly pass may be cheaper than paying per trip.
3. **A Salik tag** — only if you drive through toll gates. Registered via the Salik app.
