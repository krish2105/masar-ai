## How zones determine fare

nol fares depend on how many zones a journey crosses:

- Travel within a single zone: 1-zone fare
- Travel between two zones: 2-zone fare
- Travel across three or more zones: 3-zone fare (RTA does not price beyond three)

Gold class carriages are charged at double the standard fare.
