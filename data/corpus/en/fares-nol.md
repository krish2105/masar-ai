# nol fares

Public transport in Dubai is paid for with a nol card. The fare depends on the **number of fare zones** a journey crosses, and on the card type.

## Card types

| card | fare multiplier | card cost (AED) |
|---|---|---|
| Silver nol (silver) | ×1.0 | 25.00 |
| Gold nol (gold) | ×2.0 | 25.00 |
| Blue nol (personalised) (blue) | ×1.0 | 70.00 |
| Red ticket (red) | ×1.0 | 2.00 |

## Fare by zones crossed

| zones | standard fare (AED) |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3.00 |
| 2 | 5.00 |
| 3 | 7.50 |

Journeys crossing more than 3 zones are charged at the 3-zone rate.

## Passes

- Daily All Modes: AED 20.00
- Weekly All Zones: AED 110.00
- Monthly All Zones: AED 350.00
- Monthly One Zone: AED 230.00
- Monthly Two Zone: AED 270.00
